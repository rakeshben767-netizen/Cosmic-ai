Privacy Policy for COSMIC AI

Package ID: com.cosmicai.app · Effective Date: November 23, 2026 · Contact: watchrewards99@gmail.com

COSMIC AI ("we", "our", or "the App") is a real-time 3D voice-to-voice AI companion featuring Normal Mode and Badmash Comedy Mode. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use COSMIC AI on Android or the web.

1. Information & Permissions We Use

  • Microphone Audio (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only when you actively tap Power On / Speak to stream live voice audio for generating real-time AI voice responses. Audio is processed transiently and is not sold or shared with advertisers.
  • Contacts & Phone (READ_CONTACTS, CALL_PHONE): Optional permissions accessed strictly on-device only when you explicitly command COSMIC AI to call a contact. Your contact list is never uploaded to advertising networks or external marketing servers.
  • Local Preferences & Ad-Free Pass State: Stored locally on your device using browser/WebView storage (e.g., your 20-ad 12-hour Ad-Free Pass countdown and backend connection settings).

2. AI Processing & Third-Party Services

COSMIC AI connects to backend AI voice services powered by Google Gemini Live API to understand your spoken prompts and synthesize spoken replies. Requests are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/WSS connections.

3. Third-Party Advertising

COSMIC AI displays ads via third-party advertising partners for periodic breaks and optional Rewarded Ads (such as unlocking Badmash Mode or earning a 12-hour periodic-ad-free pass). Our advertising partners may collect device advertising identifiers, IP address, app usage diagnostics, and ad interaction metrics to serve and measure ads in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. Content Modes & Age Suitability

COSMIC AI includes an optional comedy roast persona ("Badmash Mode") intended for mature audiences (13+ / Teen & Adult comedy banter). Hate speech, protected-group slurs, explicit pornography, and threats of violence are strictly prohibited.

5. Data Retention & Security

We do not maintain user account databases or sell personal data. You can revoke Microphone, Contacts, or Phone permissions anytime from your Android device Settings, or clear local app data to erase stored preferences.

6. Children's Privacy

COSMIC AI is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Contact Us

For privacy questions, Play Store data safety inquiries, or support, email us at watchrewards99@gmail.com.
